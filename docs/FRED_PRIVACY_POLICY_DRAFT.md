# Fred Myers privacy policy content draft

Prepared: 2026-08-19. Updated for the Build 4 Golden Egg boundary on
2026-08-25. Publication and App Store Connect updates remain separate gates.

Target dedicated URL: `https://theflinsappvaultllc.com/fred-myers/privacy`.
The currently published `https://theflinsappvaultllc.com/privacy` page is
specific to Pokemon Field Academy and must not be used as Fred's App Store
privacy-policy URL.

Fred Myers and the Moonpetal Marsh stores campaign progress, settings, earned
coins, cosmetics and local scores on the player's device. The current app does
not use advertising, tracking, developer analytics, location, contacts, photos,
camera, microphone, health data, chat or user-generated content. The Flins App
Vault LLC does not receive the device-local game save in this build.

Players may use Apple Game Center when it is available. Game Center is an Apple
service governed by Apple's terms and privacy practices. Fred remains playable
as a guest when Game Center is unavailable or the player does not connect.

The current app has no in-app purchases or separate Flins App Vault account.
The paid download is processed by the App Store, and the developer does not
receive the player's full payment credentials.

Fred includes an optional hidden Golden Egg feature. A discovery remains local
unless the app has an authenticated secure connection to the official Golden
Egg service. When available, the app may send a pseudonymous player binding,
app and build version, opaque gameplay-verification evidence, request-security
information and the player's Public or Anonymous choice. The service assigns
the authoritative discovery identifier, server date and time, permanent global
and Fred-specific ranks, first-finder state, public reference, cosmetic Secret
Code and privacy status. Pending and Anonymous discoveries appear publicly as
Anonymous. A marsh profile label appears publicly only after an affirmative
public choice.

Golden Egg records are retained while the Hunt operates to preserve permanent
ranks, prevent duplicates and fraud, recover interrupted requests and honor
privacy choices. The hosting and security providers may process ordinary
network information, such as IP address, request time and connection/security
logs, solely to deliver and protect the service. A verified request to access,
correct, anonymize or delete applicable Golden Egg or support information may
be sent to the business privacy contact below.

If a later update adds a developer-operated account, cloud save, analytics,
support form or any other collection not described above, this policy and the
App Store privacy answers must be updated before that feature is enabled.

Children should not enter a real name, contact detail or other personal
information into the app. The current build does not provide chat, free-form
text entry or a developer-operated account. A parent or guardian may direct a
privacy or support question to `theflinsappvaultllc@gmail.com` and should not
send a child's personal information unless it is necessary to resolve the
request.

The policy may change when the app or legal requirements change. The effective
date must be revised for a material update, and the App Store privacy answers
must remain consistent with the shipped build.

The validated public-page candidate uses an effective date of August 25, 2026
and the approved business contact `theflinsappvaultllc@gmail.com`. Before it is
used in App Store Connect, publish the dedicated target URL, verify it in a
clean public session, and reconcile Apple's privacy answers with the exact
shipped build.
